# Svalbard: payroll withholding in 2026 v0.2

> **Source-cited draft.** Tax year 2026, checked 24 October 2026. General reference for working papers; a qualified professional must review the facts and filing position. Independent AI review does not confer accountant review.

## Who this applies to

Employers paying remuneration within [Svalbard Act §§2-1, 2-2, 3-1 and 5-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68) withhold at payment. This covers residents and nonresidents with **at least 30 continuous days** of local stay. For the temporary test, arrival/departure travel days are excluded and an absence interrupts the period; **14 days + an off-island weekend + 20 days** are two short stays ([wage guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/svalbard/skatt-lonn/)). The resident test is separate and short holidays do not necessarily interrupt residence. Svalbard only, not Jan Mayen.

## The base

Withhold on gross taxable remuneration, without personal, minimum or employment-expense deductions. Include ordinary salary, commissions, directors' fees, holiday pay from Svalbard-taxable employment, severance, taxable benefits and surplus expense allowances. Pensions and the listed social benefits follow §3-1's statutory categories; a nonresident benefit recipient must meet §2-2's local-stay and active-work-at-entitlement conditions ([Act](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68); [wage guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/svalbard/skatt-lonn/)). There are **no withholding-free months** and no Svalbard tax cards.

Value standard benefits under [2026 regulation §1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2025-12-15-2559). For subsidised housing, taxable value is the positive difference between its monthly table amount and rent paid by the employee. Add the benefit to cash pay. Tax is deducted from cash as far as it reaches; the employee pays the remaining tax to the employer when the benefit is provided (Act §5-2). For ordinary benefits not covered by that table use Income Tax Act §§5-3 and 5-12 as applicable. The regulation imports mainland rules for electronic communications, occupational car/snowmobile reimbursements and travel allowances; the exact imported 2026 allowance schedule is not verified here and remains a payroll coverage gap. Do not call an undocumented allowance tax-free.

For year-to-date taxable payments by one employer, calculate tax at the low rate to 12 G and high rate thereafter; deduct prior correctly computed year-to-date tax to obtain current withholding. Use the **last G fixed for the income year**, not average G. Salary and specified sole-trader profit share the annual band; each employer's withholding test uses that employer's own cumulative payments, while the taxpayer with combined income above the band can request advance tax (§5-2). Reconcile multiple employers and business income, rather than giving each stream a permanent 12 G allowance.

**Youth refund from 1 January 2026:** for an employee aged **13–19 at year end**, qualifying employment earnings and sickness pay up to **NOK 100,000** are exempt. Pensions and other insurance benefits do not qualify. Employers still withhold income tax and applicable insurance from the first krone. Skatteetaten refunds qualifying withheld amounts; the statutory payment is as soon as possible and no later than **three weeks after the refund decision**, not after payday (Act §3-3; Svalbard regulation §5; wage guidance). A person turning 20 during the year is outside the allowance. Report qualifying earnings correctly to support the refund.

On departure, unconditionally earned but unpaid remuneration is treated as paid at departure under [Act §3-1 and Income Tax Act §14-3(4)(b)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1999-03-26-14/KAPITTEL_14). Do not lose accrued salary or holiday pay merely because the payment follows departure. Correct under/over-withholding under Act §5-2: the employer pays a shortfall, with the stated recourse rights; an excess is repaid to the employee. Correct the reported period as well as the ledger.

## Deadlines and filing

Establish the employer organisation number and reporting access, identify the employee by Norwegian identity/D-number, register/report the employment and Svalbard income in **a-melding**, and record payment dates. File monthly by the **5th of the following month**, using the applicable non-working-day rule. Report also when Svalbard activity ceases ([Act §5-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68); [Administration Act §8-8](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14); [wage guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/svalbard/skatt-lonn/)). Do not defer reporting because payroll is small or no income tax is ultimately retained after a youth refund.

From **1 January 2026**, remit withheld tax **no later than the first working day after remuneration is paid** ([Payment Act §§10-10(1), 10-11](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2005-06-17-67)). Use the authority's payment identifier and current account instructions. Monthly reporting and payment are separate. Insurance follows the Svalbard collection rules in [regulation §5](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/1997-12-15-1305). Employer contribution is zero only in the statutory cases; mainland work requires its own treatment.

## Penalties and interest

Under [Svalbard Act §4-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68), the Tax Administration Act applies. Missing mandatory information can trigger a daily coercive fine, ordinarily **0.5 court-fee units per day**, capped at **50 units** for the ordinary information failure ([Administration Act §14-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14); [regulation §14-1-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2016-11-23-1360/KAPITTEL_13)). The authority's notice sets the start date; this is not an automatic charge on every late return.

Incorrect or incomplete information producing a tax advantage attracts **20%** additional tax, reduced to **10%** for the specified third-party-reported information. Intentional or grossly negligent failures can attract a further **20% or 40%**. Excusable circumstances, qualifying voluntary correction and other statutory exceptions must be tested ([Administration Act §§14-3 to 14-6](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2016-05-27-14)). These percentages apply to the tax advantage, not turnover.

Unpaid tax bears interest from the due date under [Payment Act §§11-1 and 11-6](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2005-06-17-67). The [authority's 2026 interest table](https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/forsinkelsesrenter/) gives **12% a year for 1 January–30 June 2026** and **12.25% for 1 July–31 December 2026**. Split arrears at the change date. A payment falling due in 2027 needs the rate then in force; do not extrapolate 2026 rates.

The employer's liability for an income-tax withholding shortfall is separately imposed by Act §5-2. A youth refund does not excuse failure to withhold/remit/report.

## Cross-border points

Assess employee duration separately from employer business activity. A Svalbard employer's work performed on Svalbard has the zero employer-contribution rate even where that employee has not met the income-tax stay test; another employer gets that rate for remuneration taxed under the Svalbard Act ([contribution decision §3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/STV/forskrift/2025-12-18-2748)). This does not remove employee insurance. Obtain the membership decision and any Nordic coordination evidence using the social guide. Mainland wages are not Svalbard wages merely because the payer or employee has a Svalbard address. Use the residence guide for departure and overlapping residence.

## Two worked examples that recompute

**Ordinary adult employee.** Monthly cash salary NOK 50,000, no benefits, member in the ordinary salary class, no youth relief, annual income below 12 G. Income tax NOK 4,000 and insurance NOK 3,800 leave NOK 42,200 cash. Employer contribution is zero on these Svalbard facts.

```oa-check
name: Svalbard adult monthly cash payroll 2026
inputs:
  cash: 50000
steps:
  income_tax: cash*0.08
  insurance: cash*0.076
  net_cash: cash-income_tax-insurance
expected:
  income_tax: 4000
  insurance: 3800
  net_cash: 42200
```

**Benefit and threshold crossing.** Same member/class assumptions; previously paid gross NOK 1.6m by this employer. Current cash NOK 60,000 plus a free car NOK 2,850 produces cumulative gross NOK 1,662,850. Only NOK 38,588 of this month's gross remains in the low band; NOK 24,262 is high-rate. Current income tax NOK 8,424.68 plus insurance NOK 4,776.60 leaves cash NOK 46,798.72. Figures are before operational final-tax rounding; income bases are whole kroner under decision §4.

```oa-check
name: Svalbard car benefit and band crossing 2026
inputs:
  previous_gross: 1600000
  cash: 60000
  car: 2850
  g: 136549
steps:
  gross: cash+car
  band: 12*g
  cumulative: previous_gross+gross
  total_tax: min(cumulative,band)*0.08+max(0,cumulative-band)*0.22
  previous_tax: min(previous_gross,band)*0.08+max(0,previous_gross-band)*0.22
  current_tax: total_tax-previous_tax
  insurance: gross*0.076
  net_cash: cash-current_tax-insurance
expected:
  current_tax: 8424.68
  insurance: 4776.60
  net_cash: 46798.72
```

## Ask the client first

- Which exact dates and work locations establish residence or the continuous stay?
- What is the employee's year-end age and insurance membership/class?
- What cash, holiday pay, directors' fees, benefits and allowances are paid or accrued?
- What has this employer and each other employer paid this year, and is there sole-trader income?
- What reports, remittances, corrections, departure payments or youth refund decisions exist?
