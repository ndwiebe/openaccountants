# Faroe Islands VAT registration and returns v0.1

> **Source-cited draft.** Professional review is pending. Figures are limited to the periods and assumptions below. This Guide prepares working papers; it is not authority to file or pay.

## Scope

Prepare ordinary Faroese MVG registration and quarterly VAT working papers in 2026. Exempt sectors, detailed place-of-supply rules, real property and import valuation require additional classification.

Sources were accessed on 22 October 2026. A dated rate table controls its stated period; an undated guidance page does not prove that all later amendments have been incorporated.

## Registration and rates

TAKS states a standard **25% MVG** rate. Its general page describes exemption below DKK 50,000 annual sales; the starting-business page uses “do not exceed”, while the foreign-registration page uses “exceeds” over twelve months. **The exact DKK 50,000 boundary is unresolved in these English pages.** Do not automate a boundary decision until the statute is checked. [General VAT](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/vat/general-about-vat); [starting a business](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/starting-a-business/starting-a-business).

Submit a Vinnuskráin application at least eight days before VAT-liable business begins. A foreign business without a Faroese place of business or residence needs a registered local representative. [Registration guidance](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/vat/how-to-register-for-vat).

## Computation

1. Classify sales by territory, taxable/exempt/export treatment and tax point.
2. Reconcile the taxable domestic sales ledger to output VAT.
3. Verify each input amount's recoverability and invoice/import evidence; private or exempt-use purchases cannot simply be included as though fully deductible.
4. Reconcile the VAT control account and submit the actual return, including nil periods where required.
5. Keep foreign representative and customs obligations separate from an income-tax PE analysis.

**Ordinary fully taxable example:** net sales DKK 100,000 × 25% = **25,000 output VAT**. Reviewed deductible input VAT DKK 10,000 gives **15,000 payable**. A VAT-inclusive price of DKK 125 contains **25 VAT**, calculated as 125 × 25/125, not 125 × 25%.

## Filing calendar and conflict

TAKS's specific quarterly list is **10 May, 24 August, 10 November and 10 February of the following year**, moving weekend/holiday dates to the next working day. The same page's general “one month and ten days” description does not explain the August exception. Retain the specific list and confirm the period's actual due date in Vinnugluggin before filing. Nil returns are required. The page specifies a late-return increment of 1%, minimum DKK 300 and maximum DKK 1,000, and unpaid-tax interest of 0.7% per month. [Quarterly returns](https://www.taks.fo/en/business/vat/quarterly-vat-returns).

**Penalty arithmetic only:** 1% of DKK 15,000 is 150, so the stated minimum gives 300; 1% of 200,000 is 2,000, capped at 1,000. Interest timing and any other charge need separate verification.

## Release gaps

Resolve the threshold boundary, statutory exceptions, input restrictions, invoice requirements and import/export evidence. Do not represent a simple output-minus-input example as coverage of all VAT transactions.
