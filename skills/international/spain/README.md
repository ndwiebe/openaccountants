# Spain: 2026 tax pack

Spain's 2026 tax pack covers common-territory rules and records the regional rules still to confirm. Select the taxpayer's residence, territory and period before choosing a Guide. The statutory routes below are the starting points; the pack is being rebuilt and is **not yet complete or independently reviewed as a pack**. No accountant has signed off this revision.

The deductions sibling now distinguishes current Madrid, Cataluña, Andalucía, Aragón, Castilla-La Mancha, Asturias, Illes Balears, Cantabria, Canarias, Castilla y León, Extremadura, La Rioja, Murcia, Galicia and Comunitat Valenciana regional credits from the national rent credit effective 8 October 2026; all fifteen common-territory catalogues are drafted, with specific unresolved interactions recorded as gaps. The quarterly-payments sibling now implements the 2026 direct-assessment payment and negative-result rules, with module-entry source conflicts and remaining transition/module computations stated explicitly. The incoming-worker sibling is now reconciled to 2026 eligibility, source deeming, ordinary/savings tax and the foreign-credit cap, with two executable examples. The remaining personal-tax siblings cover self-employment, capital gains, rentals and crypto. Their tax years and computations are part of this reconciliation. Formation, bookkeeping, e-invoicing, financial statements and tax optimisation remain separate accounting-pack work; their presence does not close a tax-domain gap. [references.md](references.md) preserves the historical research trail and is not legal authority for a figure.

Special investment, fund, cooperative, Canary Islands and sector regimes, detailed treaty-rate tables and public corporate CbCR reporting require their own verified treatment. Ordinary regional personal taxation, foral taxpayers and mixed-input VAT are recorded above where their absence affects ordinary coverage.
