Self-employed and standalone director groups have different fund coverage. Use the individual guides, including their dated rate tables and limitations, for calculation.

## Scope and follow-ups

The seven skills above are the tax computation routes. `mk-tax-overview` is a **supporting router**, not an eighth computational tax guide. It and `mk-company-formation` and `north-macedonia-tax-optimization` are preserved 2025 legacy files and have not been reverified in this wave. Do not use their figures as 2026 calculation inputs. Formation/optimisation belong to supporting or Accounting packs work, not missing first-milestone tax domains.

A separate scoped follow-up must replace the overview's secondary citations, unconfirmed minimum-global-tax assertion and oversimplified annual filing dates, then reconcile the formation and optimisation files against current primary law. The overview's corporate/PIT headlines must not override category rules, including the separate games-of-chance rate. Existing platform metadata and marker-to-EOF CTA blocks remain unchanged. Those inherited CTAs are not a claim of professional review for these drafts.

Special regulated-sector/state-aid regimes, minimum global top-up tax, gambling/unexplained-wealth assessments, specialist non-working insurance categories, VAT special schemes and product-level rate decisions, comprehensive treaty-rate tables and treaty-specific MAP limits are outside this ordinary pack. They do not establish an exemption or zero liability. The four explicit core gaps are distinct from these specialist exclusions. Business losses require a separately established relief basis; first-year professional/other unresolved contribution cases and departures require further primary-law resolution before computation.
