# Dominica: company annual returns

## Scope and separate deadlines

This Guide concerns the ordinary-company return under section 194 of the Companies Act 1994. The Act commenced on **24 April 1997** under SRO 21 of 1997. It is not a complete company-law, financial-reporting or tax-filing opinion. The sources below establish distinct obligations; paying an annual fee does not itself establish that a return was filed. [Commencement Order](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-order-1997.pdf).

| Obligation | Deadline and reference date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Company annual return | Send to the Registrar **not later than 1 April** in each year after incorporation or continuance under the Act. Information is made up to the preceding **31 December**, and the return is accompanied by prescribed fees. | [Companies Act, section 194(1)](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-act21.pdf#page=105) |
| Annual company fee | A company on the Register of Companies pays the prescribed annual company fee **on or before 2 April** each year after incorporation. | [Act 16 of 2001, section 3 inserting section 9A](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-act16-2001.pdf#page=2) |

These dates must not be collapsed into one 2 April deadline. Nor should the business-name annual-return rules be substituted for the company return. See the separate [business-registration Guide](dm-business-registration-and-tax-onboarding.md) where a company also uses a registered business name.

## Form and information

SRO 25 of 2012, effective **1 October 2012**, prescribes Form 12A for section 194. Regulation 26B requires proposed or actual business activities in company applications and annual returns to use ISIC. [2012 Regulations, regulations 1, 3 and 4](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-regulation-2012.pdf#page=1).

Form 12A requests the return date, company number and name, any different business name, registered office, separate main business address, telephone and email, business activity codes, share classes and numbers issued, shareholder names/addresses/shareholdings, and director names and addresses. Use continuation sheets where needed. The form prints 31 December as its reporting date; this is not the submission deadline. [Form 12A in the statutory Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-regulation-2012.pdf).

A director or officer must certify the return under section 194(2). Form 12A provides the name, signature and date of the director or secretary authorising it. Prepare the return from the company's records and reconcile changes during the reporting year before signature. Retain the signed return, registry receipt and separately identified payment evidence. [Section 194](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-act21.pdf#page=105); [Form 12A](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-regulation-2012.pdf).

## What this return does not settle

The annual return lists shareholders but does not by itself establish compliance with every beneficial-ownership disclosure requirement. Financial statements, audit requirements, solvency certifications, meetings, tax returns and event-driven director or office changes need their own legal checks. Section 194(3) makes non-compliance an offence for the company and each defaulting director/officer; this draft does not certify current penalty amounts. [Section 194(3)](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-act21.pdf#page=105).

The 2012 Regulations separately prescribe Form 24 for external-company returns under section 356. This Guide does not apply the ordinary-company deadline to that regime. [2012 Regulations, regulation 3](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/companies/companies-regulation-2012.pdf#page=2).
