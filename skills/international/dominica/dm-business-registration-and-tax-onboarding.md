# Dominica: business registration and tax onboarding

## Choose the registration route

CIPO distinguishes a business name from a company's corporate name. A sole trader or partnership using a name different from the proprietor names, or a company trading under a different name, falls within its business-name guidance. CIPO states that these business names must be registered. A business-name application does not establish that a company has been incorporated. Identify the legal owner before selecting forms. [CIPO business-name guidance](https://cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/business-names/).

This Guide covers domestic business names and the ordinary company application workflow. It does not certify the complete Companies Act amendment chain, beneficial-ownership duties, external-company or international-business-company regimes, all sector permissions, fees or processing times. No registration has been submitted as part of this research.

## Business-name application

The Business Names Rules, SRO 26 of 2012, commenced on **1 October 2012**. Rule 6 prescribes:

| Applicant | Form |
|---|---|
| Individual | BN1 |
| Firm or partnership | BN2 |
| Company using a business name | BN3 |

Use the forms in the [Rules' Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=5), or confirm the current equivalent with CIPO. The registry's narrative page refers to 2011 rules, while its law index links the enacted 2012 instrument; this Guide follows the retrieved instrument.

Rules 3–5 prohibit identical or misleadingly similar registered names and restrict misleading affiliations. Government or recognised university/professional-association connections need the specified written consent; the Rules also prohibit names implying political-party or leader connections. A name registered contrary to the rules can be cancelled. Keep the name decision and required consent documents. [Rules 3–5](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=1).

Rule 7 requires activity descriptions using ISIC. BN1 asks for proprietor identity/contact details, any former names, business status and start date, existing TIN, social-security employer status, intended employees, activity codes and business address. BN2 collects partner details and provides a statutory-declaration procedure if all partners do not sign. Do not omit partners or sign another person's declaration without the prescribed authority. [Rules 6–7 and Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=3).

Rule 8 permits the Registrar to send application information to Inland Revenue and, where employees are intended, Social Security. That permission is not evidence that either registration has actually completed. The scheduled declarations also acknowledge change notification and annual return/fee duties; this draft has not established their current deadlines or amounts. [Rule 8 and Schedule](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/files/business-names-rules.pdf#page=4).

## Ordinary company application

CIPO identifies **Form 1 plus Form 9A for each director** for ordinary incorporation. Its workflow allows electronic preparation but still instructs applicants to print, obtain signatures, deliver the forms and pay the registration fees. Treat data entry as an application stage; retain the issued certificate as completion evidence. [CIPO company instructions](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/companies/).

The retrieved Form 1 requests the company type, proposed name, registered office and separate business address, share classes and authorised/issued numbers, transfer restrictions, directors, activities, proposed start, financial year-end, employee/import-export intentions and incorporator signatures. It says the first financial year must not exceed twelve months. Resolve these facts before signing. This is the published form's requirement, not a completed review of every statutory accounting-period rule. [Form 1](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/forms/bnc/Application-Incorporation-companies-act.pdf).

The form requires confirmation that no incorporator is under eighteen, adjudged of unsound mind or bankrupt, and includes an undertaking to notify Social Security of employees. Its declaration uses “less than 18”, whereas the registry narrative says “over eighteen”; this draft follows the actual form wording without certifying the full eligibility law. [Form 1, declaration](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/forms/bnc/Application-Incorporation-companies-act.pdf#page=2).

## Tax and employer onboarding

CIPO says company-incorporation information is shared with Inland Revenue for automatic taxpayer registration and that tax confirmation accompanies the incorporation certificate. It separately states that taking on employees still requires **PAYE registration**. Where employees are intended, information is also shared with Social Security for employer registration. Check the actual confirmations and PAYE status rather than treating one certificate as proof of every tax enrolment. [CIPO company instructions, tax and social security](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/companies/).

For each file, retain the legal registration, tax confirmation/TIN, employer registration confirmation, PAYE registration evidence and any required activity permissions. Use the separate [VAT Guide](dm-vat.md) and [social-contribution Guide](dm-social-contributions.md) for their stated scope and unresolved checks. Business-name registration alone does not establish VAT liability or registration.

The IRD's [older startup page](https://www.ird.gov.dm/customer-service/starting-a-business) also describes business registration and activity licences, but contains an older VAT threshold. This Guide does not repeat that threshold or certify licence fees from that page. Determine permissions for the actual activity and premises separately.

## Continuing obligations and remaining work

CIPO identifies ongoing company filings including director/registered-office changes, annual returns and accounts or solvency certification. Current statutory deadlines, entity-specific exceptions, beneficial ownership and fees require further checking before a compliance calendar is certified. [CIPO company guidance](https://www.cipo.gov.dm/businesses-and-companies/companies/).
