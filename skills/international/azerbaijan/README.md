# Azerbaijan: tax pack

The State Tax Service administers the Tax Code. Ordinary income/profit tax uses the calendar year, VAT uses calendar months and payroll combines monthly payment obligations with quarterly reporting. Amounts are Azerbaijani manat (AZN) unless stated otherwise. This pack was reverified against current primary sources on 24 October 2026. Every tax domain applies; every Guide remains a tier 2 source-cited draft. Presence of a file is not complete coverage or professional review.

[az-tax-overview](az-tax-overview.md) routes questions to those Guides. Ordinary computations include business profit and losses, depreciation, simplified turnover eligibility, employment gross-to-net, employee/employer contributions, entrepreneur contributions, VAT input allocation, TP adjustments, non-resident gross-up and PE remittance tax. Each tax Guide has two recomputing examples. The partial notes identify actual missing computations, not zeros or exemptions.

PSA/pipeline and special-zone regimes, territorial/park/investment incentives, contribution-subsidy claims, specialist VAT margin schemes and country-by-country treaty rates are outside the implemented ordinary regime. Those specialist exclusions do not by themselves make a domain partial. The substantive core gaps above do.
