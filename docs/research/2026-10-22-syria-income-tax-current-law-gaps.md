# Syria income tax: current-law reconciliation

## Decision

Do not publish a 2026 income-tax rate schedule from the proposed reform. The sources retrieved on 22 October 2026 do not establish its enactment. Currency conversion also does not make a historical exemption current. This research is not accepted IT-IND, IT-CORP or payroll coverage.
