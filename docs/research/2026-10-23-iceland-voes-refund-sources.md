# Iceland VOES and separate foreign-business VAT refund: source preparation

Writer: Iceland support chat. Sources read 23 October 2026. This is a still-local addition after benefits/options snapshot `e7241273c12d006e112a7f674e65491cf92d02ee`; the benefits/options review does not cover it. All seven country domains remain partial and all Guides remain tier 2. No application, email, return or refund was submitted.

## Primary evidence read

- [VAT Act 50/1988](https://www.althingi.is/lagas/nuna/1988050.html), current 1 October 2026 compilation: Articles 5B, 14, 34(4)–(5) and 35. The scheme is an optional simplified registration for specified foreign B2C supplies, distinct from the obligation to account for VAT and the purchaser-liability route. Article 14 includes qualifying electronic books at 11%; electronic delivery alone does not establish 24%.
- [Current Icelandic registration guidance](https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/virdisaukaskattur/skraning-a-vsk-skra), operative VOES application/restriction instructions, and [registration booklet section 4.3.6](https://www.skatturinn.is/media/baeklingar/VSK_Skraning_a_vsk_skra.pdf), fully read for that section. The booklet expressly permits self-application without an Icelandic agent after simplified approval. It does not remove the representative requirement for other ordinary registration.
- [English VAT guidance](https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/value-added-tax/), VOES output-only filing, aligned two-month periods, deadline/business-day movement and ISK/dated VAT-FX instruction. The actual FX series/value and accepted taxpayer fields remain to be established when used.
- [Public VOES entry](https://voes.rsk.is/) and [application start](https://voes.rsk.is/Application/Register). The public start asks for a contact email. No address was entered and no application link was requested; subsequent required fields come from the official registration instructions, not an asserted completed application.
- [Regulation 1243/2019](https://files.reglugerd.is/pdf/1243-2019/current), all operative Articles 1–13, current reproduction effective 9 September 2022. Article 2 extends the separate refund route to Article 5B registrants and the specified nonregistrable-establishment case; Article 3 still requires all use/registration/vendor/input-entitlement conditions and excludes Iceland resale/final consumption, including its express foreign travel-operator example. The specific subcontractor/import exception is retained separately.
- [Icelandic foreign-business refund guidance](https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/skattamal/endurgreidslur-virdisaukaskatts/erlendir-adilar/) and [English refund page](https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/vat/), full operative content. Document/form/agency references were read without claiming accepted filing.

## Boundaries and source discrepancies

The current regulation and Icelandic guidance use the fifteenth of the following month for normal-cycle applications, late processing in the next cycle and an ultimate six years from the right arising. The English page instead says at least fifteen days after the period and six years after calendar-year end. Its broad no-Iceland-sales wording also omits the regulation's express simplified-registration extension. The addition states these discrepancies rather than silently harmonising them or certifying a contested operational result.

Article 8's 75,000/15,000 are December 2019 CPI 473.3 base amounts, indexed at each year's start. The English page's 101,000/20,200 are expressly 2025 amounts. The Icelandic page prints base values. No confirmed 2026 indexed minimum was found; the method requires it before submission. It does not infer a number from a historic page or guess rounding. No taxpayer refund entitlement is established by the hypothetical eligible-amount control.

The two new controls cover mixed-rate output without any VOES input offset, and disjoint exclusions from a separate refund register. Their inputs assume supported classification/use; they do not settle registration, the current minimum, claim dates, FX, actual invoices or an authority decision. These increase the country total from 64 to 66 controls. The firm Iceland source card was updated before drafting.
