# Saint Lucia company filing/payment research, 22 October 2026

Read base sections 84, 96, 113, 114, 115 and 134 from AG revised laws and Act 10 of 2026 from saved NPC PDF. New 96(3) wording is awkward: up to five months from year of assessment, subject to submission by statutory due date, no further (1) extension. Do not certify an unconditional five-month calendar without resolving application. Existing 96(2) names (1) only; Guide does not overstate it as explicitly referring to new (3). No amendment to 113/114 in Act 10.

Section 113 fixed March/June/September instalment dates, previous chargeable-income estimate, application to reduce; off-calendar-year remainder within three months. No unsupported shift of instalment months. Section 114 assessment due rule subject to 113; written payment arrangement does not change interest date. Section 134 now six percent or ministerial order, not old ten; no retroactive application inferred. Interest section 115 is distinct and orders/prime rate not verified. Amendment PDF date 21 July 2026; transitional questions retained. Sources hashed and saved; structural checks not legal review.
